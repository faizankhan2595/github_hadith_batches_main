## Grading Methodology

### Critical Rules Applied

1. **"Problematic" Grade Reserved for Liars**
   - ONLY used for narrators accused of lying (Kadhdhab, Wadda')
   - NOT used for weak narrators or disconnections

2. **Disconnections**
   - Graded as "Weak But Might Be Acceptable"
   - Example: Hadith #45307 with Abu Ubaydah → Ammar disconnection

3. **Mudallis Narrators**
   - Qatadah (category 3): Noted in potential issues
   - Al-Zuhri (category 3): Noted in potential issues
   - Impact: Does NOT severely affect grade given their high reliability (Thiqah Thabt)
   - Their use of 'an is mentioned but accepted by majority of scholars

4. **Maqtu' Narrations (94.9%)**
   - Statements of Tabi'in about Quranic exegesis
   - Graded based on chain reliability
   - "Sound" for strong chains, "Acceptable" for chains with mudallis using 'an
