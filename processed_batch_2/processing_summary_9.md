### 6. Methodology Applied

#### Narrator Grading
All narrators were graded using classical hadith criticism standards:
- Companions: Automatically "Companion" grade
- Thiqah Thabt: Highly reliable, precise narrators
- Thiqah: Reliable narrators
- Majhul: Unknown/unidentified narrators

#### Transmission Terms
Careful attention paid to:
- حَدَّثَنَا (haddathana) - strong, direct
- سَمِعَ (sami'a) - explicit hearing, strongest
- عَنْ ('an) - potentially ambiguous
- أَنَّ (anna) - often indicates indirectness
- قَالَ (qala) - could be indirect

#### IISGrade Criteria
- **Perfect**: Would require all thiqah/thabt narrators + explicit terms + marfu' + no issues
- **Sound**: Reliable narrators, minor term concerns, mawquf/maqtu' from reliable sources
- **Acceptable**: Generally reliable with some 'an usage or juristic opinions
- **Weak But Might Be Acceptable**: Disconnections or reliability concerns but not completely rejected
